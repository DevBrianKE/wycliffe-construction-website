ABOUT US

Trusted Construction & Civil Engineering Experts

Mulan Construction Company Limited is a dynamic and progressive construction firm committed to delivering high-quality, sustainable, and innovative construction solutions across Kenya.

We specialize in building works, roads, civil engineering, industrial infrastructure, consultancy services, and project management, with a strong focus on safety, quality, and client satisfaction.

Our commitment to integrity, professionalism, and excellence has enabled us to successfully undertake projects for both government authorities and private sector clients.

NCA Registered: Category NCA 8 Building Works Contractor and NCA 8 Road Works Contractor.

Membership: Member of the Roads and Civil Engineering Contractors Association (RACECA).
